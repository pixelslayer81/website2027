Design & Technology

  1. (00) Intro The AI Task Force is a cross-functional team focused on identifying, developing, and scaling AI-powered solutions that improve productivity, creativity, and business outcomes. It evaluates emerging AI technologies, builds practical tools, and helps teams adopt AI responsibly.
  2. (01) StyleShift Web App Style Shift lets you instantly restyle photos to match different brand aesthetics while keeping the original image intact. The app preserves faces, poses, objects, and composition, then checks each result for accuracy. Users can fine-tune colors and grain, create social-media crops, and save or download finished images.
  3. (02) Always On Social Always On Social turns rough updates into brand-approved social content. It creates on-brand copy, prevents unsupported claims, and generates ready-to-publish designs across major social formats. It also reviews content for compliance and supports new brand onboarding quickly.
  4. (03) Exporter Exporter is a Figma-to-Adobe Express bridge. Select a frame in Figma, press Send to Adobe Express, and seconds later it appears on your Express canvas: the artwork as a pixel-perfect image, and every text layer live and editable, matching the original font, weight, size, spacing, and alignment exactly.
  5. (04) AZURE VISUAL EXPANSION Azure Visual Expansion uses AI to scale Azure’s visual identity beyond a traditional asset library. From a small set of grayscale 3D shapes, Runway generated hundreds of new shapes, materials, colorways, and compositions, with designers guiding every direction. The result is a flexible, on-brand system that grows with Azure.
  6. (05) Excel Excel: Beyond the Grid is an interactive web experience that turns Excel into a creative canvas. Visitors explore spreadsheet art, from an ASCII globe drawn in cells to formula-built type, then paint their own cell art in a live grid and save it. It shows a playful side of Excel and invites people to experiment, share, and see the familiar grid in a new way.
  7. (06) Dotdasher Dotdasher is a pattern generator for dots and dashes. Pick a shape, choose how it repeats across the canvas, set the colours, and the pattern builds itself live. When it looks right, export it as an image or a vector at any size.
