Design & Technology

  1. (01) StyleShift Web App Style Shift lets you instantly restyle photos to match different brand aesthetics while keeping the original image intact. The app preserves faces, poses, objects, and composition, then checks each result for accuracy. Users can fine-tune colors and grain, create social-media crops, and save or download finished images.
  2. (02) Always On Social Always On Social turns rough updates into brand-approved social content. It creates on-brand copy, prevents unsupported claims, and generates ready-to-publish designs across major social formats. It also reviews content for compliance and supports new brand onboarding quickly.
  3. (03) Exporter Exporter is a Figma-to-Adobe Express bridge. Select a frame in Figma, press Send to Adobe Express, and seconds later it appears on your Express canvas: the artwork as a pixel-perfect image, and every text layer live and editable, matching the original font, weight, size, spacing, and alignment exactly.
