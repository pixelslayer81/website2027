STYLE SHIFT

Looks

The Style Shift look lineup includes Candid, a signature documentary-inspired look with on-camera flash, natural film grain, and authentic 35mm photography aesthetics; Shadows, a dramatic look focused on bold lighting and contrast; and the Pantone Collection, a series of tone-on-tone color treatments available in Green, Pink, Blue, and future colorways. These looks transform clothing, props, and background surfaces into a unified color story while preserving key elements. Skin tones, hair, outdoor skies, laptops, and paper materials remain unchanged, backgrounds are recolored rather than replaced, and each image is limited to two solid accent colors for a clean, cohesive result. New Pantone colorways can be added quickly through a simple configuration update.

Audit

Quality assurance is built into every render. An automated audit compares each result to the original image and assigns a fidelity score based on how closely key elements match. The system flags issues such as changed objects, altered backgrounds, tinted skin, camera shifts, or color-rule violations. Recolor styles use specialized checks that allow intended color changes while detecting unwanted changes to content, identity, or composition.
