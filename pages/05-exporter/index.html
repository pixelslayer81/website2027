What is it:

EXPORTER is a Figma-to-Adobe Express bridge. Select a frame in Figma, press Send to Adobe Express, and seconds later it appears on your Express canvas — the artwork as a pixel-perfect image, and every text layer live and editable, matching the original font, weight, size, spacing, and alignment exactly. No exporting files, no copy-and-paste: design in Figma, finish and localize in Express.

Why we built it:

Designers build in Figma, but the people who actually customize and ship those designs — swapping headlines, localizing copy, resizing for a channel — live in Adobe Express. Getting work between the two meant re-exporting flat images or rebuilding layouts by hand, and every handoff drifted a little from the original. We made EXPORTER so the design arrives in Express exactly as it was approved, with the type still editable, so designers stay in control of the system and everyone else can safely make it their own.
