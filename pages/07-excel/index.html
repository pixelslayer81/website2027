What is it: An interactive web experience that turns Excel into a creative canvas. Users can explore different approaches to spreadsheet art and create their own compositions using the familiar structure of Excel cells, colors, and patterns. The project takes something normally associated with data and productivity and reimagines it as a playful space for creativity, experimentation, and visual expression.

= TRIM(cell)

Why we built it: Excel is known for spreadsheets, formulas, and data, but we wanted to show a completely different side of it. By turning the familiar Excel grid into a place to make art, we created a fun and unexpected way for people to play with the brand. It’s less about teaching someone how to use Excel and more about creating a moment of discovery that gets people experimenting, sharing, and seeing Excel in a new way.
